About

Finance student, product builder, and stubborn believer in making ideas real.

Khushaank is studying ACCA with a BCom from Delhi University. His work sits between accounting, entrepreneurship, software, and the slightly messy process of figuring things out by building.

Background

He is interested in the practical side of finance: how people understand money, how small businesses make decisions, and how better tools can make those decisions less confusing.

Alongside coursework, he builds websites, prototypes, and experiments. The common thread is usefulness. A thing should solve a real problem, explain itself clearly, and respect the person using it.

Right now, the main focus is knotes.in, a platform for writing thoughts in a more open, intentional way.

Working Style

Start with the user

Useful beats flashy. The question is always what someone can do better after the product exists.

Keep the language plain

Finance and technology both get harder when the words get unnecessarily big.

Build small, then improve

Momentum matters. Ship the honest version, learn from it, and keep tightening the work.
